Incident command that builds its own interface.

Give it any incident log. Three agents investigate it, the dashboard is compiled for that incident while they work, and a decision is proposed with its sources. You approve. It dispatches and files the review.

03  compile

Each agent streams its own schema-locked components. A provisional brief lands in seconds and is replaced in place once verified.

The commander proposes. You approve or override. Dispatch to the channel, report to the feed, open the post-incident issue.
