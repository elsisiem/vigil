Incident command that builds its own interface.

Paste the log. A swarm of agents reads the readings, walks the floor plan and grounds every recommendation on the live web, then compiles the dashboard you need for this incident, component by component, while it is still thinking. You approve, it dispatches, and it files the review.

01  ingest

Any unstructured incident log: radio transcript, sensor dump, a staff message, a live feed tick. Structure is recovered, never assumed.

02  investigate

Three specialists with partial knowledge. Sensors match hazard signatures. Facility routes people to exits. Research cites OSHA, NFPA, FEMA and what is happening outside right now.

03  compile

Each specialist streams its own schema-locked components. Provisional cards appear in seconds and are replaced in place as findings are verified.

04  act

The commander decides. You approve or override. The station dispatches to the incident channel, reports to the feed and opens the post-incident review.
