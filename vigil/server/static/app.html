How Vigil works
  1. Start. Paste a log in the top bar and press Run, or pick an incident on the left. Replays need no internet.
  2. Progress. The tracker at the top right shows where the agents are: reading the log, investigating, building the brief, deciding.
  3. The call. The big card is the recommendation: contain (deal with it in place), evacuate (get people out), or observe (nothing to do). Change it with the three buttons if you disagree.
  4. Approve. Sends the alert to the incident channel and files a review in GitHub. Turn on Live to post for real; otherwise it is a safe dry run.
  5. Floor plan. Red rooms are affected, striped rooms are closed off, green rooms are safe, purple rooms are exits. Circles count people; the moving dot follows the route out.
  6. Tabs. Do now is the checklist. Options are what the research supports, each with a source. Readings, People, Activity and Sources show the detail.
  7. Update. Type what changed at the bottom right. The brief is rebuilt for the new situation.
